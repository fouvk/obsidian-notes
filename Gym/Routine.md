### **Day 1: Upper Explosive & Chest Focus (Push)**

​_Builds upper body pressing strength, chest density, and shoulder health._

1. ​**Dumbbell Incline Bench Press:** 3 sets × 8–10 reps _(rest 90s)_
2. ​**Standing Overhead Dumbbell Press:** 3 sets × 8–10 reps _(rest 90s)_
3. ​**Low-to-High Cable Flyes or Dumbbell Flyes:** 3 sets × 12–15 reps _(rest 60s)_
4. ​**Push-Ups (Explosive / Dynamic):** 3 sets × Max reps _(focus on pushing off the ground fast)_
5. ​**Triceps Cable Pushdowns:** 3 sets × 12–15 reps _(rest 60s)_

### ​**Day 2: Back, Vertical Pull & Rear Shoulders (Pull)**

​_Strengthens the posterior chain to improve shoulder stability for hitting and posturing._

1. ​**Lat Pulldown (Neutral or Wide Grip):** 3 sets × 8–10 reps _(rest 90s)_
2. ​**Seated Cable Row:** 3 sets × 10–12 reps _(rest 90s)_
3. ​**Face Pulls:** 3 sets × 15 reps _(crucial for rotator cuff protection in volleyball)_
4. ​**Dumbbell Hammer Curls:** 3 sets × 10–12 reps _(rest 60s)_
5. ​**Hanging Leg Raises or Captain's Chair:** 3 sets × 12–15 reps _(core stability)_

### ​**Day 3: Athletic Legs & Jump Power (Lower)**

​_Develops rate of force development for vertical jump height and lateral speed._

1. ​**Trap Bar Deadlift or Goblet Squat:** 3 sets × 6–8 reps _(rest 2 mins)_
2. ​**Dumbbell Bulgarian Split Squats:** 3 sets × 8–10 reps per leg _(rest 90s)_
3. ​**Romanian Deadlifts (Dumbbell):** 3 sets × 10–12 reps _(hamstring and hip hinge power)_
4. ​**Standing Calf Raises:** 4 sets × 15 reps _(fast acceleration / jump takeoff)_

### ​**Day 4: Volleyball Speed, Mobility & Active Recovery**

​_Keeps you athletic without burning out your muscle growth._

1. ​**Depth Jumps / Broad Jumps:** 3 sets × 5 reps _(maximum effort, long rest between jumps)_
2. ​**Medicine Ball Rotational Throws:** 3 sets × 8 reps per side _(hitting power)_
3. ​**Shoulder & Thoracic Spine Mobility Drills:** 15 minutes
4. ​**Light Core / Planks:** 3 sets × 45-second holds

### ​**Day 5: Upper Hypertrophy & Arms (Push + Pull)**

​_Balanced upper body volume to build size and symmetry._

1. ​**Flat Dumbbell Bench Press:** 3 sets × 8–10 reps _(rest 90s)_
2. ​**Single-Arm Dumbbell Row:** 3 sets × 10–12 reps per side _(rest 90s)_
3. ​**Dumbbell Lateral Raises:** 4 sets × 12–15 reps _(builds shoulder width)_
4. ​**Incline Dumbbell Curls:** 3 sets × 10–12 reps _(rest 60s)_
5. ​**Overhead Triceps Extension:** 3 sets × 10–12 reps _(rest 60s)_

### ​**Day 6: Lower Body Power & Core**

​_Focuses on unilateral strength and leg drive._

1. ​**Leg Press Machine:** 3 sets × 10–12 reps _(rest 90s)_
2. ​**Dumbbell Walking Lunges:** 3 sets × 10 steps per leg _(rest 90s)_
3. ​**Hamstring Curl Machine:** 3 sets × 12–15 reps _(rest 60s)_
4. ​**Ab Wheel Rollouts or Cable Woodchoppers:** 3 sets × 12 reps

### ​**Day 7: Full Active Recovery & Foam Rolling**

​_30 minutes of light walking, stretching, or light volleyball court practice._
